Intelligent Admin

Client
Healthcare
Context
Startup
Industry
Healthcare
Duration
4 weeks
Tools
Figma, Claude

Problem

Hospital administrators lack a unified operational view of hospital activities, making it difficult to identify resource bottlenecks, coordinate departments, and respond quickly to operational issues.

My Role

UI/UX Designer

Process

Analyzed existing hospital administration workflows, identified operational bottlenecks and fragmented information flow, then restructured the experience into a role-based AI-assisted operations platform with a unified information hierarchy.

Key Decisions

Hospital administrators don't need to continuously monitor dashboards. They need the system to surface critical operational issues automatically so they can focus on running the hospital instead of watching metrics.

Outcome

The redesign changed the administrator's workflow from reactive monitoring to proactive operations management. Rather than manually identifying issues across multiple modules, administrators are guided toward the most critical operational events first.

Thali

Context
Personal

Problem

Event hosts in India have no way to find, compare, or book a caterer who understands their regional cuisine — so every booking defaults to whoever a relative recommended, at whatever price they quoted.

No platform exists that speaks the language of Indian event food: region, sequence, cultural context. The result is opaque pricing, unverifiable quality, and menus that feel generic instead of authentic.

My Role

Product Designer

Process

Audited existing catering platforms and mapped their UX against how Indian event hosts actually plan food — found a complete mismatch between restaurant-model interfaces and the regional, sequential nature of Indian event catering. Conducted observational research across family events to document the real decision flow. Identified three structural gaps: no regional intelligence, no cultural menu sequencing, no reverse-bid price discovery. Restructured the information architecture around four mental stages instead of eleven linear steps, reducing cognitive load without losing functionality. Designed the menu builder to follow cultural sequence — sweets before mains — and built the dynamic thali as a functional feedback mechanism, not decoration. Applied a reverse-bid model over fixed pricing because event catering pricing is always contextual, never commodity.

Key Decisions

The core USP of THALI is that the host builds the exact menu first — every dish, every category — and only then do caterers receive that specific menu as a bid brief. Vendors price each dish individually against the same menu, so the host sees a true like-for-like comparison: why Caterer A costs more, exactly which dishes drive that difference, and whether it's worth it. No other platform does this — everywhere else you pick a vendor first and negotiate food and price separately, blind.

Outcome

For the first time, an event host can see exactly what they're paying for — dish by dish, vendor by vendor — and make a confident decision without a single phone call.
