Three problems, solved end to end.

01

Thali

Personal · Product Designer

Problem
Event hosts in India have no way to find, compare, or book a caterer who understands their regional cuisine, so every booking defaults to whoever a relative recommended, at whatever price they quoted.
Process
Audited catering platforms and observed real family events. Found restaurant-model interfaces clash with the regional, sequential way Indian events plan food. Restructured the flow around four mental stages instead of eleven linear steps.
Key decision
The host builds the exact menu first. Only then do caterers bid on that menu, dish by dish, so comparisons are truly like-for-like.
Outcome
A host can see exactly what they're paying for, vendor by vendor, and decide confidently without a single phone call.
02

Intelligent Admin

Healthcare startup · UI/UX Designer · 4 weeks

Anonymized under NDA
Problem
Hospital administrators lack a unified operational view, making it hard to spot bottlenecks, coordinate departments, and respond quickly.
Process
Analyzed existing admin workflows and fragmented information flow, then restructured the experience into a role-based, AI-assisted operations platform.
Key decision
Administrators shouldn't watch dashboards. The system surfaces critical issues so they can run the hospital instead.
Outcome
The workflow shifted from reactive monitoring to proactive operations management, guiding admins to the most critical events first.
03

True Communication

Anonymized under NDA
Problem

Process
